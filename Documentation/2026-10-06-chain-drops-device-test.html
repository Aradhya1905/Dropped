Findings, most severe first
BugMapScreen.tsx:220

The range card hides the next-stop card and offers to re-open read drops

nearestInRange = drops.find(s => isWithin(coord, …)) doesn't skip drops you've already read. Right after you reveal stop N you're still inside its 50 m, so the bottom slot shows "Trail stop 1 · tap to break the seal" for a stop that's already open (screenshot 9), and showNextCard stays false. The NextStopCard never appeared once in the whole run. It only shows after you walk 50 m away from every drop.

It's also not the nearest: find returns the first match in list order. That's why the card showed a month-old drop while stop 1 was closer.

const nearestInRange = drops
  .filter(s => s.sealed && isWithin(coord, s.drop.coordinate))
  .sort((a, b) => haversineMeters(coord, a.drop.coordinate) - haversineMeters(coord, b.drop.coordinate))[0];
Needs a fingerRubReveal.tsx · PanResponder

Rubbing stops after the first few pixels

With adb-injected swipes, only the touch-down point gets rubbed (screenshot 6). I added temporary logs and got grant → move → move → terminate after about 12 px, in every direction. onPanResponderTerminationRequest was never called, so a native view is stealing the touch. It isn't the ScrollView: with scrollEnabled=false its onInterceptTouchEvent returns early. My best guess is the gesture-handler root view.

Why it matters for trails: NextStopTicket only renders once rubDone is true, so if this also happens with a real finger, nobody can continue a trail from a fresh reveal. Please rub once by hand to confirm. I reverted the logging.

The detail sheet still shows a revealed stop as "sealed · walk here to unlock" with "1 found" (screenshot 8). Going back to the Walk screen after a reveal still says "Break the seal".
